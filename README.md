## Frozen Bottle

### What I worked on

At Frozen Bottle, I worked closely with the **creative and social media agency** to plan and execute monthly campaigns around product launches, offers, and brand moments.

My role involved working with the agency on **content ideas, briefs, and performance feedback**. We used trends, audience behaviour, and content performance to understand what was working and improve the next campaign.

We created campaign-led Reels using **trending audio, viral hooks, and creative storytelling**, while keeping the content connected to the wider marketing calendar.

Alongside social content, I also helped build a direct brand community for **offers, launches, and retention**.

### Results

- **24K → 60K** Instagram followers
- **36K+** net follower growth
- **150%** follower growth in 12 months
- **50M+** cumulative Reel views
- **2 Reels** crossed 20M+ views
- **6 Reels** crossed 1M+ views
- **4,000+** community members built in 8 months

### How I approached it

**Campaign → Creative → Distribution → Performance Signals → Optimisation → Community**

The focus wasn't just on publishing content. It was about understanding **which ideas, formats, hooks, and trends made people stop and engage**, and using those learnings in the next campaign.

## Cozywalks

### What I worked on

For Cozywalks, I worked on building the brand's social presence around its **modular sneaker concept, sustainability, and product innovation**.

The content focused on making the product easier to understand through Reels, Stories, educational content, and behind-the-scenes storytelling.

### Results

- **1M+ Instagram reach** in 30 days
- **220 → 1,000+** followers
- **355%** follower growth in one month
- Generated pre-order enquiries through **Instagram DMs and Stories**
- Supported the launch of the **Carbon Infinity Ultra Modular Sneaker**

### Content approach

**Product Education → Storytelling → Reels → Stories → Engagement → Enquiries**
